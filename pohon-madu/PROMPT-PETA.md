# Prompt pembuatan peta jelajah (gaya sama dengan Desa Akar / Rumah Nenek / Dojo)

Tempel **Gaya dasar** + **isi peta** ke pembuat gambar. Bahasa Inggris dipakai karena biasanya hasilnya lebih patuh.
Ukuran: **1536×1024** (sama dengan peta yang sudah ada); area luas boleh 2048×1365.

## Gaya dasar (selalu disertakan)
```
Top-down 3/4 isometric view (camera tilted about 45°), detailed painterly pixel art game map, cozy fantasy JRPG
environment, same style as a hand-painted village map with stone paths, wooden fences, lush green trees and mossy rocks,
warm sunlight from the upper left with soft shadows, rich saturated colors, high detail.
Empty scene: NO characters, NO animals, NO creatures, NO text, NO labels, NO UI, NO border.
Wide clear walkable dirt/stone paths (paths at least 1/7 of image height wide). Obstacles (trees, rocks, water, cliffs,
walls) clearly distinct from walkable ground. Scale: a house door is about 1/10 of the image height.
Landscape 1536x1024.
```

## Prolog
**4. Hutan Pinggir** (paling dibutuhkan)
```
Forest edge map. A winding dirt path enters from the BOTTOM-LEFT corner and exits at the TOP-RIGHT corner.
3 small open grassy clearings beside the path. 2 short dead-end side paths: one ends at a big hollow tree with a
glowing honey pot inside the hollow, one ends at a flowering bush. Big leafy trees, ferns, mossy rocks, an old
broken wooden fence, a fallen log, wildflowers, a wasp nest hanging from one tree. Midday sunbeams through leaves.
```

## Bab 1
**5. Padang Ilalang**
```
Wide golden tall-grass meadow under bright afternoon sun. A dirt road crosses the map from the LEFT-BOTTOM edge
to the RIGHT edge. Gentle hills, one big lonely tree, haystacks, scattered rocks, a small wooden signpost (blank).
3 open trampled-grass clearings near the road.
```
**6. Desa Kerikil**
```
Small village built of grey stone: stone houses with flat slate or thatch roofs, cobblestone streets, a stone well
in the central square. An inn with a blank hanging sign, a shop stall, a stonemason workshop with blocks and tools.
Two houses have open doors with scattered belongings outside and small muddy footprints leading out of the village.
Roads exit at the LEFT edge and the TOP edge.
```
**7. Lembah Kerikil**
```
Rocky valley path winding between grey cliffs and large boulders. A small waterfall and stream with a little wooden
footbridge. Path enters from the BOTTOM, exits at the TOP-RIGHT; a side branch on the TOP-LEFT leads to a dark old
mine entrance framed with wooden beams and a lantern.
```
**8. Jembatan Batu**
```
A wide blue river crossing the map diagonally, with a long arched stone bridge in the middle. At the near end of the
bridge: a small wooden guard post with a red flag and a big round shield hanging on its wall, and an open dirt
clearing (arena). Path enters BOTTOM-LEFT, continues across the bridge to the TOP-RIGHT. Late afternoon light.
```
**9. Tambang Tua – Lantai 1**
```
Inside an old mine, dark brown cave walls lit by warm hanging lanterns. Wooden support beams, mine-cart rails with
an ore cart, crossroads of 2-3 tunnels, one dead-end tunnel with a small wooden chest. Entrance from the BOTTOM;
a wooden ladder/lift going down at the TOP-RIGHT.
```
**10. Tambang Tua – Lantai 2**
```
Deeper mine cavern, darker, glowing purple crystals, small pools of water, a plank bridge over a narrow chasm.
A medium chamber full of sharp stone spikes in the middle. At the far TOP end a big hideout chamber with piles of
stolen goods: sacks, crates, red cloth, torches. Ladder up at the BOTTOM-LEFT.
```

## Bab 2
**11. Desa Embun**
```
Misty morning village of wooden stilt houses over shallow water, connected by plank walkways. An open-air kitchen
stall with a big cooking pot, steam rising and a dining bench. A village spring in the square with MUDDY BROWN water.
Inn, ingredient shop, a house with a sickbed on its porch. Walkways exit LEFT and RIGHT.
(Also make a second version with the spring CLEAR BLUE and sparkling.)
```
**12. Rawa Kabut**
```
Foggy grey-green swamp. Zig-zag wooden plank boardwalks over murky water, small grassy islands, dead trees, reeds,
purple flowers, mossy stones, a patch of shimmering silver-leaf bushes and a clump of ginger plants.
A large dark pond on one side. Boardwalk enters LEFT, exits TOP-RIGHT.
```
**13. Gua Embun**
```
Blue glowing crystal cave with a stream, flat stepping stones and a small waterfall. A path winding downward deeper.
At the far end a large chamber where a spring is clogged with a big pile of brown mud. Entrance BOTTOM-LEFT.
```
